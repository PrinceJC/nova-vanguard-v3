Account support

Help with signing in, verification codes and your saved progress.

I can’t sign in

Nova Vanguard uses one-time codes instead of passwords. On the start screen choose Sign in, or Can’t sign in? Recover your account, and enter the email address or mobile number on your account. A new code arrives within a minute. Check your spam folder for emails.

The code didn’t arrive

Wait for the resend timer (60 seconds) and tap Resend code. Codes expire after a short time, so use the newest one. For text messages, make sure the country code is correct.

I no longer have access to my email or phone

Contact us from another address with your username and the approximate date you created the account. We’ll verify ownership before changing anything.

Progress and usernames

Progress is saved to your account after every cleared level and follows you to any device you sign in on. Changing your username never affects progress or scores.
