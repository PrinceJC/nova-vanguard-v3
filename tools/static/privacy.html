Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026. Site owner: review this text with your own legal advisor before launch.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address or mobile number (used only to send sign-in codes) and the username you choose.
  • Game data: campaign progress, settings, selected avatar, and endless-mode runs (score, survival time and the gameplay events used to verify them).

What others can see

Your username, avatar and verified endless scores appear on public leaderboards. Your email address and phone number are never shown to other players.

How it’s stored

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Supabase). Sign-in codes are delivered by our email and SMS providers. We don’t sell personal data or show ads.

Your choices

You can change your username at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us through the account support page.

Children

If you are under the age required by your country to consent to online services, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.
